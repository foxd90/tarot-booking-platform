Understanding
Tarot — Honestly
What Tarot Is
  • A metaphorical instrument for accessing deeper self-knowledge
  • Support for uncovering what you truly want and need
  • A source of imagination, inspiration, and insight
  • A companion through life's uncertainties
What Tarot Is Not
  • A precise fortune-telling tool
  • Rooted in determinism — it honours personal agency and free will
  • A crutch to depend on, rather than a tool for growth
  • Something to fear — it is neither dark magic nor a shortcut to easy answers
The Guide
About Cawa

I am Cawa Liss — a storyteller, guide, and magician based in Berlin. My primary instrument is the tarot deck, which I approach not as a mystical oracle, but as a profound psychological tool — not unlike a Rorschach test for the soul. Your truth lives within you. My role is simply to help you hear it.

I hold training in Reiki, Shamanism, and the Jungian interpretation of Tarot, and I am currently studying Buddhist Psychology and Herbal Medicine. Every reading I offer is shaped by a deep commitment to your empowerment.
