About this service

What is a Sunlit Sojourn?

By default this is a remote service. If you’d prefer an in-person call with Cawa, simply note it in the booking description.

This reading maps how the coming year will shape each area of your life, and offers guidance on how to tip the scales in your favour. For each life area, the following is explored: current state, obstacles & challenges, hopes & fears, past influences, allies & supporting environment, external influences ahead, and advice for the year.
