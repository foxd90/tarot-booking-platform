About this service

What is a Clarity reading?

By default this is a remote service. If you'd prefer an in-person call with Cawa, simply specify it in the booking description.

This reading is designed to shed light on a specific, timely question in a particular area of life — for example: "Why is my dating life challenging and how to make it better?" or "What is the source of my anxiety and how best to support myself?"

As many cards as necessary will be drawn to gain a clear overview and guidance. Minimum 3 cards.

Your question

How to Ask a Good Question

Clearly state your question in the booking form. You don't need to share every detail — just indicate the area and what you'd like explored.

Good examples
Challenges in my love life and how to overcome them.
How can I be more successful at work and earn a promotion?
Which of my pursuits — teaching, writing, or astrology — will bring the most fulfilment?

What to avoid: Questions about other people's feelings or fixed future outcomes. If your question needs refinement, we'll be in touch with alternatives.
